How this map is calculated
  • A scheduled script, not your browser, retrieves the data and does the calculations: recent readings from ECCC’s real-time hydrometric service, and history from ECCC’s approved national archive (HYDAT). The map only reads the resulting files.
  • The user can choose between two precomputed historical distributions: same calendar date or a seasonal window around that date (daily means from a range of days centred on the comparison date across usable historical years).
  • The recent value is a rolling 24-hour time-weighted mean ending at the latest reading, and needs at least 18 hours of readings. The 24-hour minimum and maximum are shown without a percentile because the archive holds daily means only.
  • A percentile needs at least 10 years of record for that station and comparison method. Water level uses only years recorded on the same datum (reference level) as today’s reading. Readings older than 48 hours are treated as stale and not compared.
  • Percentile colour belongs to the station point only. Watershed polygons remain neutral geographic context and are never coloured from a single station measurement.
  • Temperature appears only where a hydrometric station publishes a direct sensor channel, and without a percentile, because no historical temperature record is available. No weather-station substitution or modelled water temperature is used.
Data: Environment and Climate Change Canada real-time hydrometric data (provisional and subject to revision) and the HYDAT national archive. Published-measurement statuses come from a dated check of ECCC and WSA sources. Map geometry: simplified from official Saskatchewan Water Security Agency province-boundary and watershed exports.
